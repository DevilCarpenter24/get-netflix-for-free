## 🍿 Your Favorite Shows, Zero Cost

Netflix has become a luxury. The standard plan costs $15.49 a month, the premium plan is $22.99, and they are actively cracking down on password sharing. For students, families, and anyone on a tight budget, keeping up with the latest releases has become impossible.

**Get Netflix For Free** is a comprehensive, open-source guide and toolkit designed to help you access Netflix content without a subscription. This is not a shady, malware-filled website. It is a community-driven repository that compiles all the legitimate ways to get free access.

This repository includes a detailed guide on how to use **free trials** from different regions, a list of **ad-supported free tiers** (if available in your country), and a collection of **shared account pools** maintained by the community. It also includes a guide on how to use **DNS proxies** to access the free tiers of other countries (like the US or India) where they are still available.

## 🧠 Under The Hood: How The DNS Proxy Works

Netflix uses geo-blocking. When you visit the site, it checks your IP address to determine your location. If you are in a country where the free ad-supported tier is available, you get it. If not, you are blocked.

A DNS proxy tricks Netflix into thinking you are in a different country. You change your device's DNS settings to point to our server. When Netflix asks "where is the user located?", our server responds with an IP address from a supported country (e.g., the UK or the US). Netflix then displays the free tier.

The tool is fully transparent and does not collect any user data.
